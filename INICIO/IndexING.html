What is our goal?

Awareness

We share information about the different types of cancer and how to support a family member going through the illness.

Real support

We offer financial and psychological guidance to those who don't have many resources to cover their treatment.

Privacy first

We respect the privacy of every person who seeks help on our site, without exception.
