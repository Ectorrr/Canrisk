About us

Canrisk is an informative website that shares accessible, reliable information about the most common types of cancer in the Salvadoran population: breast, cervical, prostate, stomach, and lung cancer.

Our project is called Canrisk because it comes from the combination of two important words: cancer and risk. We fused both words to give the site a name that's easy to remember and approachable.

What drives us

Our mission

Bring clear, reliable medical information about breast, cervical, prostate, stomach, and lung cancer to the Salvadoran population, without unnecessary jargon.

Our approach

We support both patients and their families: prevention, emotional support, and practical guidance, all in one place.

Our commitment

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Anything you share with us is handled with the utmost care.
